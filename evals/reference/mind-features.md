**Signs in without a password.** A person authenticates with a one-time code sent to their email or through Google, never a stored password. Issued sessions are tracked individually and can be revoked one at a time rather than all at once.

**Pairs a headset to a practice.** Devices register themselves and are bound to the person using them, so a session knows which hardware is attached and what it can report.

**Calibrates neurofeedback per person.** Calibration measurements are recorded and kept against the individual, so feedback thresholds follow that person's own baseline instead of a fixed constant shared by everyone.

**Runs guided practice sessions.** Breathing exercises are delivered with graded difficulty and alongside meditation poses, and free-form notes can be attached to an individual segment of a session rather than only to the session as a whole. A session can be shared publicly.

**Streams biometrics and cues live.** While a session runs, readings flow continuously from the device and instructions flow back to it, both as open streams rather than as separate requests.

**Serves incremental change feeds.** A client asks for everything that changed since it last synchronised and receives only the delta; the change log behind it is pruned as entries age.

**Tracks practice statistics as sessions end.** Totals update the moment a session reaches its end state, and sessions that were completed, abandoned, and interrupted are counted apart from one another rather than lumped together.

**Speaks to a person in their own language.** Transactional email follows the locale stored on the profile, and in-app release announcements are served per language, so both channels reach a person in the language they chose.
